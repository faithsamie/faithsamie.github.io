# Editing style guide: faith__creator short-form

Taken from three reference screen recordings (Nov 2026). The skills (Remotion, HyperFrames, sound-effects) should
follow this guide when making videos in this style.

## Format
- Vertical 9:16 (1080x1920), 30–60 fps. Clips run about 6–15 s.
- Talking head, framed from the chest up and at eye level. Warm indoor light: a lamp glow or orange wall behind.
- A handheld lav mic stays visible in frame as part of the look.
- The talking-head take is usually continuous with no jump cuts. Pace comes from fast speech and overlays, not cuts.

## Layer 1: hook title (0 s → about 3 s)
- Font: condensed heavy all-caps sans (Bebas Neue / Anton feel). White, 2 lines, centred near the top
  (about 6–10% from the top edge).
- Copy is a claim or a question: "I REMADE A VIRAL AD WITH AI", "CAN AI PASS FOR A REAL PERSON?"
- Optional series tag above it: "DAY 38/50" in black text on a white rectangle.
- Optional proof pill: a black rounded rectangle with an eye icon and "2.1M" in bold white. It sits left-aligned under the title
  and pops in about 1 s after the start, with a quick fade and scale over about 0.25 s.
- The title fades out (about 0.3 s) once the hook line is spoken, around 2.7–3.5 s. Other badges can stay a little longer.

## Layer 2: word-by-word captions (whole video)
- One word on screen at a time, synced to speech. Each word holds until the next one appears, with no animation between them.
- Font: light/regular display serif (Cormorant Garamond / Playfair feel), cream or warm off-white. No box,
  no stroke, small (about 5% of frame height).
- Position: left edge, vertically mid-frame (about 40–60% of the height), beside the face, not over it.
- These captions stay quiet and elegant, a deliberate contrast with the loud hook title.

## Layer 3: typewriter notes (the "how" section)
- After the hook, key facts type out character by character (about 12–15 chars/s) in a thin white sans at the top left.
- Each line replaces the previous one, like running notes:
  "I used Google Flow + CapCut" → "Consistent character" → "Consistent voice" → "This was the result".
- The last line sets up the reveal.

## Layer 4: payoff / B-roll
- **Full-screen reveal** (AI-ad style): blur and slightly darken the talking head over about 0.4 s, then hard cut to
  full-screen footage. The footage is cinematic and cut fast (about 1 s per shot), with motion inside the frame (whip pans,
  people bumping, an object flying toward camera).
- **Card demo** (product/website style): a screen recording inside a rounded card with a soft shadow, filling the
  top third of the frame. The face stays visible underneath. The card fades or slides in, scrolls, and cross-dissolves
  (about 0.3 s) to the next screen.

## Audio
- Voice is loud and up front (about −11 to −19 dB mean in the references).
- The music and sound effects could not be isolated from the screen recordings. Suggested: a subtle whoosh on the blur→reveal
  cut, a soft pop when the proof pill appears, light keyboard ticks under the typewriter text, and a low music bed.
